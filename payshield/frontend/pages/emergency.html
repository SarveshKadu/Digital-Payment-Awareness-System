🚨 EMERGENCY GUIDANCE

I Think I've Been Scammed

Stay calm. Follow these steps right away. This page provides general guidance only — always follow instructions from your bank or official cybercrime authority.

1

Stop communicating with the suspected scammer

Do not reply, call back, or click any further links they send. End all contact immediately.

2

Contact your bank/payment provider through an official channel

Use the number printed on your card, passbook, or the bank's verified website — never a number given to you by the suspected scammer.

3

Preserve transaction details and evidence

Save screenshots, SMS, transaction IDs, and any messages or numbers involved. This will help investigation and reporting.

4

Report the incident through the appropriate official cybercrime reporting channel

Most countries have a dedicated cybercrime reporting portal or helpline — report there as soon as possible.

5

Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords with anyone claiming to help

Even after a scam, do not share further credentials — legitimate banks and cybercrime officials will never ask for these.

PayShield does not collect banking credentials, OTPs, PINs, CVVs or any sensitive financial information — on this page or anywhere else on the platform.
